CAX SSG uses Markdown for content and YAML frontmatter for metadata. This separation keeps your content clean and your data organized. Define titles, descriptions, tags, layouts, and custom variables — all right at the top of your markdown files.

Frontmatter variables are accessible in templates via `{{ variable }}` syntax.
