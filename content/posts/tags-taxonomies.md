CAX SSG automatically generates tag pages from frontmatter tags. Define `tags: ["c", "ssg", "performance"]` and CAX creates `/tags/c/`, `/tags/ssg/`, and `/tags/performance/` automatically.

Tag pages help readers discover related content and improve site navigation.
