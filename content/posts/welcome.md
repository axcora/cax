Welcome to CAX SSG — a static site generator that strips away the complexity and gives you pure speed. Written entirely in C, CAX SSG compiles your content into HTML in milliseconds, not minutes. No Node.js, no Python, no Ruby. Just a single binary and your ideas.

This is the first step toward a faster, simpler web.
